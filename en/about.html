Yushufang is a place for research and writing by Liu Yibu. It explores how our circumstances form, how options become available, and how major family decisions shape long-term trajectories.

Before the choice

We ask not only how to choose among available options, but why these are the options available to us — and how action today might change the options available in the future.

The author and the book

Liu Yibu is the researcher and writer behind Yushufang, and the author of the work in progress 《改命》 (Changing Fate). The main body of work is written in Chinese.

Method and limits

The essays distinguish facts, the author’s interpretations and open questions. The framework does not promise predictions or guaranteed outcomes.
